The first launch compiles the shaders and can take a minute or two. Later visits load much faster.
Tip

Arrows or W S A D fly, Q E rudder, PgUp PgDn throttle, F flaps, G gear, B brakes, C camera, Esc menu. A gamepad or joystick works too (remap in the menu); on a phone use the stick and the throttle slider.

The 777 has an autopilot (Shift+A): speed, heading, altitude and vertical speed, and APP flies the ILS to 28L or 28R down to the flare and rollout.

Every landing is scored on touchdown rate, centreline and touchdown zone; R replays the flight with the director's cameras.
